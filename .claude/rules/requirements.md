# Requirements

How a conversation becomes something buildable.

## Capture
- **Who** has the problem, **what** they do today, **why** it hurts, **how** they will know it is solved.
- Record the client's words as the client's words. Paraphrase only when labelled as a paraphrase.
- Separate three piles: **said** (quote or close paraphrase), **inferred** (your reading, labelled "our guess, confirm or correct"), **open** (questions nobody answered yet).

## Acceptance criteria
- Given / When / Then, one observable outcome each. If it cannot be checked by a person or a test, it is not a criterion yet.
- Edge cases belong in criteria: empty input, very long input, special characters, double submit, refresh mid-flow, no permission, slow network, two tabs.
- "Out of scope" is a section, not an afterthought. It protects the estimate.

## Discipline
- Never put a requirement in the client's mouth. "They probably want X" is a question for them, not a line in the spec.
- An inference that becomes the base of an artifact gets a first block asking the stakeholder to confirm or correct it.
- A wrong claim is fixed everywhere it spread (spec, tickets, notes) in one pass, with a visible "corrected" note.
- Dates, names and IDs on every decision. Lead with the decision needed.
